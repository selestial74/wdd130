Pasig City

"Pasig city is a highly-urbanized city in the National Capital Region of the Philippines. It is located along the eastern border of Metro Manila and it borders Rizal province, the city shares its name with the Pasig River. A formerly rural settlement, Pasig is primarily residential and industrial, but has been becoming increasingly commercial in recent years, particularly after the construction of the Ortigas Center business district in its west."
